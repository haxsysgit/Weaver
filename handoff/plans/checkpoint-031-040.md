# Checkpoint 031 to 040 (standing ritual, no plan number)

Runs after Plan 040 closes and before Plan 041 starts. Same ritual as
`docs/checkpoints/021-030.md`.

## Goal

Step back after ten plans: learn what worked and what did not, bring the
methods up to date, write the changes into the process docs, and prove the
updated method on one real slice.

## What we want to achieve

- Walk through Plans 031 to 040 (including 033.5, 033.6 and 033.7): what
  each set out to do, what it achieved, what it cost, what failed and why.
- Update method knowledge from the web (agent evaluation, retrieval,
  long-context prompting, 3D web performance, hosting) where it bears on how
  Weaver is built.
- Codify what changed in the process docs: `AGENTS.md`, the `arinze-plans`
  skill, the plan template, the testing-realism lessons. Lessons from this
  stretch to consider: keep the PC awake for live runs; diagnose from
  transcripts before fixing; heuristics on reader text need negative tests
  from real transcripts; shell escapes and line endings on this checkout.
- Prove the updated method with one real slice of work.

## What success looks like

- `docs/checkpoints/031-040.md` records the walk-through, the method
  updates, the doc changes and the proof slice.
- Commits use the prefix `Checkpoint 040: `, with no attribution.
- The owner has read it before Plan 041 is admitted.
