## What the check set is

Six fixed questions from Plan 033, asked three times each (18 answers),
judged blind by a separate agent.

- **Scene questions (r15, r35, n18).** The reader describes a moment
  loosely; Weaver must land on that exact scene. Gate: at most 1 of 9
  answers presents a different scene.
- **Trial questions (t1, t2, t3).** The old retrieval regression set: a
  canon reconstruction, a time-aware power comparison and a canon-grounded
  what-if. Gate: at least 6 of 9 answers correct.

History: Plan 032 7 of 10 trial answers; 033 round 2, 0 of 9 substitutions
and 3 of 9 correct; 034, 3 of 9 substitutions and 2 of 9 correct.

## Evidence at drafting (observations from the 034 check run, `fa33d74`)

- **Weaver stops early, with budget left.** Scene answers used 2 of 16
  lookups (r15 under `scene_explanation`), 2 to 4 of 4 under
  `quick_recall`. Comparison answers used 5 or 6 of 12; one (t3 run 3) used
  2 of 12 and cited no chapter. The budget did not run out; the agent chose
  to answer.
- **Scene questions route to `quick_recall`** (4 lookups) in seven of nine
  answers.
- **Substitution shape:** two r15 answers cite only the earlier, better
  known scene (ch. 689) instead of the one described (ch. 871); one r35
  answer cites ch. 1580 and denies the event in ch. 2976. The third r15
  answer and two r35 answers found the right chapter with the same tools.
- **Comparison shape:** t2 and t3 answers say plainly that one side's key
  facts are missing, then reason around the gap. The notebook cards behind
  `who_is` stop at chapter 1000; the 034 name index lists where a name
  appears but not which chapters show its powers.
- **The records cannot say why.** Check records keep tool names only, not
  arguments or results, so a retrieval miss (the right chapter never came
  up), a reading miss (it came up and was not opened) and an answer miss
  (it was opened and not used) look the same.

Hypotheses (not yet confirmed): the agent's stopping rule accepts the first
plausible chapter; scene questions with distinguishing details are routed
as quick recall; comparison questions have no step that gathers each side's
defining passages before weighing them.

## Scope

- **See the misses.** Record each check turn's tool arguments and the
  chapters each tool returned (private, under `.weaver/`). Classify all 18
  answers as retrieval, reading or answer misses, with the deciding chapter
  for each question confirmed against the source.
- **Fix the measured layer, not the guessed one.** Candidates, chosen only
  after the classification:
  - a check before answering that the read chapter matches the reader's
    distinguishing details, and a second look when it does not;
  - routing for scene descriptions that carry specific details;
  - for comparisons, gather each side's defining passages first (the name
    index's chapters plus search), then weigh;
  - spend the budget the skill already has before settling, when the
    evidence is thin.
- **Prove it generalises.** A fresh held-out set (new questions, never seen
  by the fix) in the same two shapes, scored blind.
- Deterministic tests for each change, written red first, from the traced
  shapes rather than the question text.

## Out of scope

- Rewriting the meaning or word search indexes (033 and 033.5 own them).
- Extending the notebook past chapter 1000 (a reading run of its own).
- Voice, UI, deploy, tag, push, merge.

## Deterministic proof / verification floor

1. `uv run pytest -q` passes at or above the floor at admission.
2. `uvx ruff check` adds no finding on changed files.
3. `npx vitest run`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run build` pass.
4. Check set, three runs, judged blind: at most 1 of 9 substitutions and at
   least 6 of 9 trial answers correct.
5. The held-out set scores at least as well as the 034 build on the same
   questions (both arms run in this plan).
6. One twelve-trial run on the final build: no trial regresses from its
   last recorded verdict.
7. A fresh reviewer agent reads the diff; findings repaired or recorded in
   `review-ledger.md`.

## STOP conditions

- A fix keys on a check set question, scene, chapter or character.
- Failures move between runs after a fix (overfitting the diagnostic set):
  stop tuning, go to the held-out set.
- Per-turn cost rises more than 50% without a gate win.
- Metered spend would pass the $4.00 cap.
- A new test needs the real `.env` or a live model.
