## What it is

The last part of the approved design
(`deliverables/036-into-the-spell/design.md`, sections 7.4, 7.8, 10):
the fanfic room, sound, the Blender stills, and the final phone pass.

## The owner's direction so far (all 2026-11-18)

- A weave is Weaver making something new: in its room the threads turn
  gold and slow like a loom, and the presence opens into a frame around
  the story.
- Sound is off by default and switched on in Settings: a low hum in the
  void, a soft thread pluck when Weaver starts answering, a rising shimmer
  when a citation thread draws. Never autoplays.
- Blender stills replace the code-rendered frame on the Still level; also
  the app icon and link preview image.
- Phones get as much optimisation as they need.
