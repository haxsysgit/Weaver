# Plan 037.1: Home and the first visit

Status: idea. Number set by the owner (2026-11-18); nothing runs from this
doc. Drafted into a plan when Plan 037 closes.
Opened: 2026-11-18
Depends on: Plan 037 closed (the room, the scene kit, the two-layer
folders, quality levels).

## What it is

The rest of the new face around the room, from the approved design
(`deliverables/036-into-the-spell/design.md`, sections 7.2, 7.5, 7.6, 7.7,
9): Weaver opens on the Soul Sea, a new way of getting around, the first
visit restaged, and Settings rebuilt from scratch.

## The owner's direction so far (all 2026-11-18)

- Home is the Soul Sea: a calm dark sea under one black sun with a gold
  rim. Chats are memory fragments (silver lights); fanfic threads are
  weaves (gold lights). The composer starts a fragment; "Weave a story"
  starts a weave; "return to your last fragment" reopens the latest.
- Pinned first, then most recent by last activity; archived never on the
  sea; about five lights on a phone, eight on a big screen.
- The rail is retired on every screen size. In the room, the Weaver mark
  goes back to the sea; a fragments drawer (left edge or a key on
  desktop, swipe on a phone) jumps between fragments; "all fragments" is
  the full list with search, pin, rename, archive, delete, save as
  Markdown.
- The first visit keeps access (shared allowance or own key) and the
  DeepSeek notice with consent, adds "how far have you read" (with skip),
  drops the Appraisal; spoken in the Spell's rune voice over the void;
  ends on the sea.
- Settings: Reading (spoiler boundary, text size), Motion and sound,
  Your key and model (folded away), Your data, First visit. Gone: four
  themes, glass, density, Soul Sea choice, Star web, Appraisal.
- Two changes below the face: `bootstrap()` in `useChatController`
  stops auto-opening or creating a thread; the server adds a last-activity
  time to the conversation list and orders by it (the only server change,
  with backend tests).
- Blender makes the Soul Sea art (sky, sun, water) as images and
  textures; never official or ripped art.
- Split: each slice marked flash or claude. Likely flash: the engine and
  server changes, Settings, the drawer and list, the first-visit steps.
  Likely claude: the sea scene, the Blender art, the first-visit
  choreography.
