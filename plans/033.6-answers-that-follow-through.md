## Scope

- **Diagnose first.** For every turn in the 033.5 run that ended in the gap
  answer, ran over two minutes, or carried a failed trial's error, read the
  stored run events and the private transcript and record which check or
  call failed and why (counts and check names only in committed files).
- **A deadline on every model call and every turn.** A stall ends cleanly,
  with a retryable state and a short honest message, inside a bound the
  reader can live with (proposal: 90 seconds per call, 4 minutes per turn,
  set from the measured 033.5 durations). Retries stay inside the turn's
  bound.
- **Answer from what was read.** Fix the cause the diagnosis finds, with
  the target that a turn which opened relevant chapters (this turn or
  earlier in the conversation) answers from them; the gap answer stays for
  turns that truly found nothing.
- **Read to the resolution.** A question about a fight, scene, or arc reads
  forward until the scene resolves before saying how it ends, who won, or
  that something does not happen. Stated as a general reading rule in the
  prompt or skill, matching AGENTS.md ("read each chapter to its end"),
  plus a check where a deterministic one is possible.
- **Canon stays canon.** A specific scene, vision, or line presented as
  canon must come from opened text or be labelled as a read; fix the answer
  check that let the vague ask's claim through, and catch announcements of
  edits to the private draft.
- **Regression cases from the real failures**, replayed deterministically
  where the failure allows it (no novel text in committed fixtures).
- **Then all twelve Reader Experience Trials once**, and the Plan 033 check
  set in arm A.

  product could do: record it and ask before building around it.

- Live spend would pass $2.70 (cap $3.00).
- A trial fails in slice 7: record it, finish the run, no tuning and no
  re-run inside this plan.
