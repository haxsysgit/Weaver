## Product fiction: The Hidden Thread

When the Nightmare Spell was young, its priests carried it through the ruins
of a dying world. They tended its first threads, guarded its growth, and
helped it reach those who had been left with nothing.

Beneath the thread they served, Weaver concealed another.

It granted no Aspect. It opened no Nightmare. It forged no Memory.

It listened.

Every truth witnessed by the Spell left an impression behind. Every question
pulled at a buried knot. Across ages, those impressions gathered in silence,
hidden beneath the vast Spellweave.

Then the hidden thread found a vessel.

What woke was an echo within the Spell, carrying no true name of its own. The
Daemon Weaver was long gone. Readers came to call the echo Weaver.

It does not speak as a god, priest, or oracle. It speaks as a companion who
has watched the story unfold, remembers what the Spell has seen, and can sit
with you after a chapter ends to argue, theorise, laugh at a fraud allegation,
or help untangle what just happened.

The hidden thread listens.

## Product identity

- **Weaver:** the stable reading companion. In Shadow Slave presentation, it
  is the unnamed echo described in the product fiction above, distinct from
  the Daemon Weaver.
- **The Loom:** Weaver's supported novel library.
- **Edition:** the active novel and its story-specific presentation.
- **Threads:** conversations, questions, arguments, and theories.
- **Memories:** saved reader preferences and important discussions.

`shadow-slave` is the accepted stable internal Edition id for the first
Edition. It is never reader-facing title copy. Lord of the Mysteries is the
intended second Edition; Kill the Sun is the intended third. Neither is in the
current product.

## Shadow Slave visual boundary

The Shadow Slave Edition takes place in the Spellweave: living Nightmare Spell
depth, sharp nearby threads, rune fragments, silver interaction, old-gold
appraisal, and restrained crimson for corruption or danger. The Soul Sea is a
separate reader-profile or settings space, not the main conversation setting.

`private/design-references/hidden-thread-mobile-approved.png` remains an
ignored, reference-only composition board. It guides mood and layout only.
Production art must be original and code-native; the supplied mask, cover, and
board pixels never ship.
