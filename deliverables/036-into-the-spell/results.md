## Slice 4 groundwork: two Blender spikes (throwaway)

Private renders in `private/design-references/036/spikes/`.

1. Stars joined to their nearest neighbours by silver strings: reads as a
   generic network wallpaper.
2. A woven surface with stars at the crossings, haze and glow: reads as an
   80s wireframe grid.

Lesson: a literal geometric translation of the canon looks generic. What the
canon stresses is fineness, scale, light, danger in looking too long, and a
Spell that answers. The directions are defined by moments of experience
first, geometry second.

## Slice 5: Brainstorm (2026-11-18)

- **Owner's pick: blend A, B and D.** B (the Soul Sea) is home; A (the
  void) is the room you talk in; D (the woven presence with seven embers)
  is Weaver's presence and its thinking state. C's place scenes wait for art.
- **Owner's correction:** "scrolling back" means scrolling up through the
  old messages of a chat. Behind older messages the weave runs backwards
  (the reversed dream of ch. 2) and runs forward again on the way back
  down; older stretches are marked by when they were said, not by chapter.
- **Far future, not 036:** a storytelling mode where Weaver walks the reader

- Prototype of the blended room: `private/design-labs/036/e-room.html`
  (normal and `?older`), images `shots/e1-*` and `shots/e2-*`.

### Owner decisions, 2026-11-18 (second round)

- **Weaver opens on the Soul Sea.** Home is the sea; from there the reader
  starts a new chat or returns to the last one.
- **Naming (owner's concept):** Weaver's memory is the whole world of the
  novel; each chat is a **memory fragment**, a recollection where Weaver
  recalls the novel and talks with the reader.
- **Scope: Weaver is a novel lorekeeper, not only for Shadow Slave.** Lord
  of the Mysteries is the likely next novel, and its world would come in as
  a theme the reader can switch to. 036 builds Weaver's own look plus the
  Shadow Slave world only.
- **No game layer in 036.** A random First Nightmare with Aspects, Soul Sea
  types and legacies is out of scope; it goes to `TODO_future.md` at close.
- My two calls stand (owner agreed): fanfic is gold and chat is silver; a
  citation thread draws once, then fades.
- **Two layers (owner, 2026-11-18):** Weaver's own layer (threads, the
  woven presence, memory fragments, weaves, citation threads) stays the
  same for every novel; the novel layer (Shadow Slave now: Soul Sea home,
  the void, the Spell's runes) is a theme.
- **The novel is inspiration, not a blueprint (owner, 2026-11-18).** The
  design takes the feel from the chapters and invents freely, especially
  where the novel says nothing. Canon rules still apply to what Weaver
  *says* about the story, not to how the app looks.
- **How to build (owner, 2026-11-18):** a new face on the same chat engine
  (approach 1), with the React scene kit (React Three Fiber, drei,
  postprocessing) over three.js. Every screen is redrawn; the chat engine
  (streaming, threads, citations, boundary, reader memory, saving) stays.
- **Animation (owner, 2026-11-18):** both libraries, one job each. motion
  for the interface (messages, panels, layout), GSAP for the story moments
  (citation thread, rune text, first visit, scrolling back). Never both on
  the same element. Baseline today (gzip): main chunk 125 KB, three.js
  scene chunk 131 KB (already loaded lazily), motion features 16 KB,
  CSS 32 KB. A weight budget per slice is the builder's to keep.
