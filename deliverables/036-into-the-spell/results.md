## Slice 2: Read the Spell (2026-11-18)

Exact search over the shelf (3246 chapters), then the matching chapters read
around each hit. 21 chapters opened so far, within the 60-chapter bound:
2, 3, 15, 16, 17, 174, 218, 278, 351, 430, 484, 550, 743, 1162, 1169, 1204,
1751, 1840, 2410, 2412, 3109. Private notes with chapter and line references:
`private/design-references/036/canon-notes.md` (not committed).

What the novel shows, in the executor's words:

1. **The void between dream and reality** (chs. 15, 218, 351, 743): a black
   void of countless stars, the stars as nexuses of a vast net of silver
   strings forming constellations; it reads like a living neural network;
   staring at it too long is dangerous.
2. **The Spell's voice** (chs. 15, 17, 218, 278): faintly shining runes in
   the reader's sight, bracketed lines, appraisal and ranks, riddle-like
   descriptions; it whispers and keeps tactful silences.
3. **The Soul Sea** (chs. 16, 278): a calm dark sea under a single sun (the
   soul core), with spheres of light (Memories) floating around it.
4. **Spellweave** (chs. 278, 550): inside enchanted things, embers ringed by
   impossibly fine woven strings; things dissolve into white sparks that flow
   like water.
5. **The Strings of Fate** (ch. 430): a tapestry connecting everything,
   overwhelming to look at.
6. **Weaver** (chs. 278, 2410): the mask, fearsome and somber, bestial and
   regal; golden light in cold darkness.
7. **Places**: the owner's saved images of the Chained Isles and the
   Forgotten Shore.

## Slice 4 groundwork: two Blender spikes (throwaway)

Private renders in `private/design-references/036/spikes/`.

1. Stars joined to their nearest neighbours by silver strings: reads as a
   generic network wallpaper.
2. A woven surface with stars at the crossings, haze and glow: reads as an
   80s wireframe grid.

Lesson: a literal geometric translation of the canon looks generic. What the
canon stresses is fineness, scale, light, danger in looking too long, and a
Spell that answers. The directions are defined by moments of experience
first, geometry second.
