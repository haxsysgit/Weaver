# Owner Decision

## Status

**Accepted and closed 2026-09-08.**

## Decision

The owner accepted the repaired mobile and desktop experience and directed the
team to finish Plan 027.5, deploy it, tag it, and release Weaver v1.2.1.

## Accepted result

- Conversations become usable on the dark void before the Three.js Star Web
  renderer starts.
- The existing Spellweave appears only after its first rendered frame and has
  a safe SVG fallback.
- New Weave and mobile thread recall follow the accepted server-backed
  behavior and focus the composer correctly.
- The installed PWA keeps full-window coverage and opens Settings without
  waiting for the preference request.
- Quiet, Balanced, and Vivid remain distinct, and the accepted transparent
  chamber, Soul Seas, First Nightmare rite, Archive, and icon set remain
  intact.
- The composer retains its behavior and gains the accepted near-black wood and
  restrained rune-cut treatment.

The full Star Web formation effect, where stars explode from a central knot
and weave themselves into the final background, remains reserved for Plan
029.5. It is not part of this hotfix.
