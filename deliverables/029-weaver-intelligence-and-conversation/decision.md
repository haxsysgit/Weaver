# Plan 029 Owner Decision

## Status

**Accepted and closed 2026-10-17.**

The owner accepted Plan 029 after reviewing the implementation report, bounded
live evidence, complete verification floor, and both independent reviews. The
accepted result gives Weaver a source-first fiction reading path, compact
conversation continuity, an ephemeral evidence board, answer modes, packaged
identity and soul, and deterministic publication checks that fail closed when
direct evidence is absent or unsafe.

The owner also accepted two explicit contract amendments:

1. The semantic claim inspector remains a private evaluation tool. Live runs
   produced false and malformed findings, so it does not add another paid,
   slow production model call or block reader answers automatically.
2. The checkpoint-three compact turn brief remains the final design. It keeps
   the actual ask, prior positions, version signals, challenge state, and
   canonical item links. The reading loop resolves ambiguous names, character
   versions, and timelines from source evidence, with Plan 030 responsible for
   held-out proof.

The final floor passed 628 Python tests, 132 frontend tests, Ruff, the root
production build, whitespace checks, wheel-resource proof, two independent
reviews, and a staged-file privacy audit. Accounted Plan 029 live spend was
$1.164388 against the approved $3.00 ceiling. Commit `0485c23` records the
accepted implementation before this close ceremony.

Closing Plan 029 confirms the reading and conversation system only. It cannot
qualify the production DeepSeek model, accept the Foundational Reader Trials,
set public allowances, deploy, or publish v2.0.0.
